## Overrides vs Universal

| Universal default | Tsavo Rising override |
|---|---|
| Sale Completion lag | **180 Business Days** following Anticipated Practical Completion Date. Almost double the standard. |
| Late payment grace | **7 BUSINESS DAYS** (not calendar). The strictest of any project. |
| Negotiation Period (pre-enforcement) | **Disapplied where the breach is non-payment of Purchase Price.** Termination flows immediately into the Vendor's Default Notice. The Negotiation Period only applies to non-payment breaches. |
| Service Charge frequency | **Bi-annually** (not monthly). Definition reads "a bi-annually sum that shall be payable after the Anticipated Practical Completion Date". |
| Tenant-in-occupation handover | **Required.** Where Purchaser completes payment and a tenant is in occupation, Vendor must give the tenant at least 30 days' notice to vacate before official handover. Insert sub-clause in Section 4 (Conditions for Possession). |
| Vendor execution: Common Seal | **Power of Attorney variant.** |

## Drafter's Note

Tsavo Rising is the **highest-risk project for the Purchaser** of the six in the registry, and the most Vendor-protective. The 180 BD Sale Completion lag means a Purchaser who pays off the full balance could wait 9 months for title. The 7-business-day grace and the disapplied negotiation period for non-payment mean a Purchaser who misses a single instalment can be in fundamental breach within a fortnight. This is the only project where the Vendor reserved both *bi-annual* Service Charge collection and *tenant handover* protocols, indicating MAK anticipated rent-collecting Purchasers and structured around it. When advising a Purchaser at this project, **always flag these timing exposures in the engagement letter**.
