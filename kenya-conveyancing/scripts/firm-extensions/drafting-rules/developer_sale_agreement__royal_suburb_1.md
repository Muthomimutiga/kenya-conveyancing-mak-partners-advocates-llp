## Overrides vs Universal

| Universal default | Royal Suburb 1 override |
|---|---|
| Dispute resolution: NCIA mediation → court | **Arbitration before single arbitrator appointed by Chairman, CIArb Kenya Branch.** Seat: Nairobi. Language: English. Rules: Arbitration Act, 1995. Insert the CIArb variant of the dispute clause. |
| LSK Conditions of Sale 2015: omitted | **Incorporated.** Insert the standalone "LAW SOCIETY CONDITIONS FOR SALE (2015)" clause after Warranties. |
| COVID Audio-Visual Verification clause: included | **Omitted.** (Older template predates this clause.) |
| Late payment grace: per project | 60 calendar days. |
| Resale admin fee: omitted | **3% of Purchase Price** payable to Vendor on approved resale. Insert resale admin fee sub-clause in General Conditions. |
| Stage 2 electricity meter line item | **Omitted entirely.** Royal Suburb 1 bundles the electricity meter into the water meter cost. Schedule of Legal Costs has no electricity meter row. |

## Specific Recital Note

The land at Nairobi/Block 138/841 is held **freehold** by the Vendor (not leasehold from Government of Kenya as in later projects). The recital reads:

> The Vendor is the legal owner of ALL THAT parcel of land situate in the city of Nairobi in Nairobi County of the Republic of Kenya known as Nairobi/Block 138/841 (hereinafter collectively referred to as "the Land").

Followed by acknowledgement that subdivision and transfer of a portion is contemplated and the Development has been constructed on the Land per Building Plans.

## Vendor Execution

**Common Seal** variant. The Vendor signs:

> SEALED with the Common Seal of ROYAL SUBURB APARTMENTS LIMITED in the presence of: [Director] [Director/Secretary]

Not the Power of Attorney variant used in the later projects.

## Vendor's Office Email Note

This template explicitly cites `invest@tsavo.ke` as both the Architect's contact email and the Vendor's notice email — preserve this dual reference in the Architect definition.

## Drafter's Note

Royal Suburb 1 is the **oldest template** in MAK's developer portfolio. It predates the NCIA mediation pattern, predates the COVID AV verification clause, and uses the more traditional CIArb arbitration route. It also bundles electricity into water (older infrastructure setup) and carries an explicit 3% resale admin fee that the newer projects dropped.

When in doubt about whether a "modern" clause applies here, default to **omit** unless the universal explicitly says it must be present regardless of template age.
