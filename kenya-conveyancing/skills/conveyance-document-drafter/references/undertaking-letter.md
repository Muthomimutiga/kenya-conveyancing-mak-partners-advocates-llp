# Undertaking Letter — Purchaser's Advocate Undertaking

## Output Category

**Category A — Letterhead Document.** Begin output with:
`[LETTERHEAD DOCUMENT — Print on MAK & Partners Advocates LLP letterhead]`

**Typography:** Book Antiqua 12pt, single spaced, justified.

---

## When to Use

The undertaking letter is issued by the Purchaser's Advocates to the Vendor's Advocates confirming that the balance of the Purchase Price is held in the firm's client account and will be released only against delivery of the registered Transfer and new Certificate of Title.

This gives the Vendor's Advocates assurance that funds are available, while protecting the Purchaser from releasing payment before the title is registered in the Purchaser's name.

**This is a professional undertaking.** It creates a binding personal obligation on the firm (not just the client). It must be honoured. Do not issue this unless funds are actually held in the client account.

**LSK Condition 8.3(c):** An irrevocable professional undertaking by the Purchaser's Advocates confirming funds have been placed towards payment of the balance of the purchase price is one of the acceptable forms of payment on Completion.

## Undertaking Letter — Template

```
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[PRINT ON FIRM LETTERHEAD — MAK & PARTNERS ADVOCATES LLP]
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[DATE]

[VENDOR'S ADVOCATE FIRM NAME]
[ADDRESS]
[TOWN]

Dear [VENDOR'S ADVOCATE NAME / "Sirs"],

RE: [PROPERTY DESCRIPTION — e.g., "LAND REFERENCE NUMBER 209/4491 —
    TRANSFER FROM [VENDOR NAME] TO [PURCHASER NAME]"]
    PURCHASER'S ADVOCATES' UNDERTAKING

We act for [PURCHASER FULL NAME(S)] ("the Purchaser") in connection with
the purchase of the above-captioned property from your client,
[VENDOR FULL NAME(S)] ("the Vendor").

We hereby IRREVOCABLY UNDERTAKE as follows:

1. We confirm that we hold in our client account the sum of Kenya Shillings
   [BALANCE IN WORDS] (Kshs. [BALANCE IN FIGURES]) ("the Balance"),
   being the balance of the Purchase Price of Kenya Shillings
   [PURCHASE PRICE IN WORDS] (Kshs. [PURCHASE PRICE IN FIGURES]),
   in cleared funds on behalf of our client.

2. We undertake to hold the Balance in our client account and to release
   the same to you immediately upon receipt of the following documents,
   duly executed, stamped, and ready for registration:

   (a) The original Certificate of Title / Certificate of Lease for the
       Property endorsed with or accompanied by the registered Transfer
       (LRA 33 / LRA 63) in favour of our client; OR the Dealing Number
       confirming registration of the Transfer at the Land Registry; and

   (b) All other Completion Documents as specified in the Completion
       Notice dated [DATE OF COMPLETION NOTICE], including [list key
       documents relevant to the matter].

3. We undertake to present the Transfer instrument for registration at the
   Land Registry with due diligence following Completion, and to hold the
   Balance in our client account until registration is confirmed.

4. This undertaking is irrevocable and is given by this firm in our
   professional capacity as Advocates of the High Court of Kenya. It
   creates a binding personal obligation on this firm, independent of
   any instructions from our client.

5. [Where financier is involved:] The above funds are held on behalf of
   [FINANCIER / MORTGAGEE NAME], and this undertaking is given subject
   to the terms of our instructions from [FINANCIER NAME]. In the event
   of any conflict, [FINANCIER NAME]'s instructions shall prevail.

This undertaking shall be effective from the date hereof until [LONG STOP
DATE — typically 30 days after Completion Date].

Yours faithfully,

_______________________
[SIGNING ADVOCATE]
MAK & PARTNERS ADVOCATES LLP
ADVOCATES FOR THE PURCHASER
```

---

## Drafting Notes

- This is a professional undertaking — the firm (not the client) is personally liable to honour it.
- Only issue when funds are actually in the client account and cleared.
- The "long stop date" should allow enough time for registration. 30 days from Completion Date is standard.
- Where the Purchaser is being financed by a bank/mortgage, the undertaking is often given by the Financier's Advocates (the bank's lawyers) directly to the Vendor's Advocates. In that case, our firm may not issue this undertaking — check the financing arrangements first.
- After issuing, record in Airtable Documents table: Document Type = "Undertaking", Status = "Sent".
