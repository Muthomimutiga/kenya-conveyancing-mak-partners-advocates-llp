# Rates Clearance Application — Template

## Output Category

**Category A — Letterhead Document.** Begin output with:
`[LETTERHEAD DOCUMENT — Print on MAK & Partners Advocates LLP letterhead]`

**Typography:** Lexend 12pt, single spaced, justified.

---

## When to Use

A Rates Clearance Certificate (also called a Land Rates Clearance Certificate) is issued by the County Government confirming that all land rates due on a property have been fully paid. It is a mandatory Completion Document (LSK Condition 8.4(c)).

The certificate must be valid for at least 21 days from the Completion Date and accompanied by receipts showing payment for the last 3 years.

**Nairobi City County:** Apply online via the Nairobi City County e-payment portal (ecitizen.nairobi.go.ke or revenue.nairobi.go.ke). Certificate issued electronically. The process requires: property LR number, Vendor's ID, rates account number.

**Other counties:** Physical application to the County Land Rates office or County Revenue office. Templates below cover both.

## Nairobi City County — Online Portal Note

For Nairobi properties, advise the Vendor (in a separate client note or via the client update letter) as follows:

"For the Rates Clearance Certificate in respect of your Nairobi property, you will need to apply online through the Nairobi City County revenue portal. We will require the following from you to process the application:

- The property's LR Number / Title Number
- Your National ID number
- Your rates account / customer number (available on any previous rates receipts)
- All outstanding rates to be paid before the certificate can be issued

We recommend applying at least 3–5 business days before the Completion Date to allow time for processing. The certificate will be issued electronically."

---

## Drafting Notes

- After rates clearance is received, verify: (a) property description matches the title; (b) validity period covers Completion Date + at least 21 days.
- Attach 3 years of rates receipts at Completion — the Vendor should hold these. If receipts are lost, a statement of account from the County is an acceptable alternative.
- Record in Airtable: Document Type = "Rates Clearance Application", Status = "Sent" once sent; update to Status = "Ready" when certificate received.
