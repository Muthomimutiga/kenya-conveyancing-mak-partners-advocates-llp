# Requisitions on Title — Template

## Output Category

**Category B — Registry Document.** Begin output with:
`[REGISTRY DOCUMENT — No letterhead. File at Land Registry. Check title details before filing.]`

**Typography:** Book Antiqua 12pt, single spaced, justified.

---

## When to Use

Requisitions on Title are raised by the Purchaser's Advocates after the Vendor delivers the title documents. They are formal written questions requiring the Vendor to clarify or resolve issues arising from the title before the Purchaser commits to Completion.

**Timeline (LSK Condition 6):**
- Vendor delivers title documents within 14 days of the Agreement
- Purchaser has 14 days to raise requisitions
- Vendor must answer all requisitions in writing within 14 days of receiving them
- Time is of the essence for answering requisitions (LSK Condition 6.3.5)

Where the Vendor fails to answer requisitions or cannot satisfy them: LSK Condition 6.4 (Rescission) applies.

## Requisitions on Title — Template

```
IN THE MATTER OF:

[FULL PROPERTY DESCRIPTION — e.g., "Land Reference Number 209/4491" or
"Title Number Nairobi/Block 123/456"] situate at [PHYSICAL LOCATION]
("the Property")

AND IN THE MATTER OF THE PROPOSED PURCHASE BY [PURCHASER FULL NAME(S)]
FROM [VENDOR FULL NAME(S)]

REQUISITIONS ON TITLE

We, MAK & PARTNERS ADVOCATES LLP, acting for and on behalf of the
Purchaser, hereby raise the following Requisitions on Title, to which
we require written replies within fourteen (14) days pursuant to Condition
6 of the Law Society of Kenya Conditions of Sale (2015 Edition):

---

TITLE

1. Please produce the original Certificate of Title / Certificate of Lease
   for the Property. Confirm that the registered proprietor is [VENDOR
   FULL NAME(S)] and that the registered details match the description in
   the Agreement for Sale.

2. Please confirm:
   (a) the exact title description as it appears on the register;
   (b) the registered area and dimensions of the Property;
   (c) the land use category noted on the register (residential /
       commercial / agricultural / industrial).

3. Please confirm that there have been no changes to the registered area
   or description of the Property since the date of the Official Search
   Certificate issued on [OFFICIAL SEARCH DATE].

---

ENCUMBRANCES

4. [If charges noted on the Official Search:] The Official Search reveals
   a Charge in favour of [CHARGEE NAME] registered as Dealing No.
   ..................... Please confirm:
   (a) the outstanding amount secured by the Charge; and
   (b) the arrangements that will be made to discharge the Charge at or
       before Completion, including production of a duly executed LRA 58
       (Discharge of Charge) from [CHARGEE NAME].

5. [If caution(s) noted:] The Official Search reveals a Caution lodged by
   [CAUTIONER NAME]. Please explain the nature of the claim underlying
   the Caution and confirm when it will be removed prior to Completion.

6. [If restrictions noted:] Please explain the nature of any restrictions
   noted on the register and confirm they will not impede transfer of
   title to the Purchaser.

7. [If no encumbrances:] The Official Search reveals no charges, cautions,
   restrictions, or inhibitions on the register. Please confirm this is
   correct and that no further dealings affecting the title have been
   presented to the registry since the search date.

---

OUTGOINGS

8. Please produce:
   (a) An original Rates Clearance Certificate issued by [COUNTY GOVERNMENT
       NAME] confirming that all land rates have been fully paid, such
       certificate to be valid for at least twenty-one (21) days from the
       proposed Completion Date; and
   (b) Receipts evidencing payment of land rates for the last three (3)
       years.

9. [Leasehold only:] Please produce:
   (a) An original Land Rent Clearance Certificate issued by the National
       Land Commission confirming that all land rent has been fully paid;
       and
   (b) Receipts evidencing payment of land rent for the last three (3)
       years.

10. Please confirm that all utility bills for the Property (electricity,
    water) have been paid up to the Completion Date and produce the latest
    paid utility statements.

---

CONSENTS

11. [If leasehold:] Please produce the original Consent to Transfer from
    the National Land Commission (NLC) in respect of the proposed dealing.

12. [If agricultural freehold:] Please produce the original Consent from
    the Land Control Board in respect of the proposed dealing.

13. Please confirm that all other consents or approvals required by
    applicable law or by the terms of any lease or encumbrance affecting
    the Property have been obtained and will be produced at Completion.

---

POSSESSION AND CONDITION

14. Please confirm that vacant possession of the Property will be given to
    the Purchaser on the Completion Date, free from any tenancies,
    licences, or rights of occupation by any third party.

    [If subject to tenancy:] Please produce all tenancy agreements currently
    affecting the Property and confirm the terms on which vacant possession
    will be given.

15. Please confirm that no structural alterations, developments, or
    subdivisions have been carried out on the Property that are not
    reflected in the register or do not have the requisite approvals.

---

VENDOR'S IDENTITY AND AUTHORITY

16. Please produce:
    (a) A copy of the Vendor's National Identity Card or Passport (certified
        true copy);
    (b) A copy of the Vendor's KRA PIN certificate;
    (c) Three (3) coloured passport-size photographs of the Vendor.

17. [If Vendor is a Company:] Please produce:
    (a) A certified copy of the Certificate of Registration / Incorporation;
    (b) A CR12 (extract from the Registrar of Companies) confirming the
        current directors and shareholders;
    (c) A copy of the Board Resolution authorising the sale and execution
        of the Transfer;
    (d) Identity Cards, PIN certificates, and three (3) passport photographs
        of each Director.

18. [Where the Vendor is or may be married, or the Property is or may be
    matrimonial property:] Please confirm whether the Vendor is married and,
    if so, produce the executed Spousal Consent to this transaction. The
    spousal interest is an overriding interest (s.28 Land Registration Act
    2012) and binds whether or not an LRA 10 (Spouse's Interest) restriction
    is noted on the register. Statutory basis: s.93 Land Registration Act
    2012, read with s.12 Matrimonial Property Act 2013 (Article 45(3) of the
    Constitution).

---

COMPLETION ARRANGEMENTS

19. Please confirm the proposed date, time, and venue for Completion.

20. Please confirm that all Completion Documents as specified in Condition
    8.4 of the LSK Conditions of Sale (2015 Edition) will be available and
    produced on the Completion Date.
```

## Drafting Notes

- Raise only the requisitions that are relevant to the specific matter. Remove inapplicable requisitions before sending (e.g., remove leasehold requisitions for freehold transactions; remove LCB consent requisitions for non-agricultural land).
- The Vendor's Advocates must answer all requisitions in writing (LSK Condition 6.4). A blanket "no comment" or failure to answer within 14 days entitles the Purchaser to rescind (LSK Condition 6.4).
- If the Official Search (LRA 85) reveals unexpected encumbrances not disclosed in the Agreement, those must be specifically flagged and resolved before the Purchaser proceeds.
- After raising requisitions, update the Airtable Documents table — Document Type = "Requisitions on Title", Status = "Sent".
