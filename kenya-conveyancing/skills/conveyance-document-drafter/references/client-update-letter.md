# Client Update Letter — Templates by Milestone

## Output Category

**Category A — Letterhead Document.** Begin output with:
`[LETTERHEAD DOCUMENT — Print on MAK & Partners Advocates LLP letterhead]`

**Typography:** Lexend 12pt, single spaced, justified.

---

## Tone and Style

These letters go to the client — not to other lawyers. Write in plain, clear English. No unexplained legal jargon. The client is a person who has agreed to buy or sell property. They want to know:
1. What has been done
2. What is happening next
3. What (if anything) they need to do or provide
4. Any urgent matter requiring their attention

Keep each letter concise. One page maximum.

## Body Templates by Milestone

### Milestone 1 — Instructions Received + Agreement Signed

```
Thank you for instructing us to act for you in connection with the
[purchase / sale] of the above property.

We have received your instructions and have drafted the Agreement for Sale
for your review and execution. Please find the Agreement attached / [we
will share the Agreement with you separately for your signature].

Once the Agreement is executed by both parties and the deposit of Kenya
Shillings [DEPOSIT AMOUNT] has been received, we will commence the
conveyancing process in earnest.

The overall target for completion of this transaction is [COMPLETION DATE].

We will keep you updated as we progress through each stage.
```

---

### Milestone 2 — Official Search Lodged

```
We write to update you on progress with your [purchase / sale] of the
above property.

We have submitted an application to the Land Registry for an Official
Search of the title. The search confirms the current state of the register
— that is, who is registered as owner and whether there are any mortgages,
cautions, or other matters affecting the title.

We expect to receive the search result within 5 business days. We will
review it carefully and advise you of the outcome.

Note: The Official Search is valid for 30 days. We will work to advance
the transaction within that window.

No action is required from you at this stage.
```

---

### Milestone 3 — Consent Application Submitted

```
We write to update you on progress with your [purchase / sale] of the
above property.

We have submitted an application to the [Land Control Board / National Land
Commission] for the consent required to proceed with this transaction.
This consent is a legal requirement for this type of property.

[LCB: The Land Control Board meets monthly and we anticipate receiving
consent within 4 to 6 weeks.]
[NLC: We expect to receive the consent within 3 to 4 weeks. We have also
applied simultaneously for the Land Rent Clearance Certificate.]

We are also progressing the Rates Clearance Certificate with the County
Government in parallel.

No action is required from you at this stage.
```

---

### Milestone 4 — Rates Clearance Certificate Received

```
We write to update you on progress with your [purchase / sale] of the
above property.

We are pleased to confirm that we have received the Rates Clearance
Certificate from [COUNTY GOVERNMENT NAME], confirming that all land rates
on the property are fully paid.

[If awaiting consent:] We are continuing to pursue the [Land Control Board

[If all clearances received:] All the required clearances are now in order
and we are progressing towards preparation of the Transfer documents.

No action is required from you at this stage.
```

---

### Milestone 6 — Stamp Duty

```
We write to update you on progress with your [purchase / sale] of the
above property.

We have reached the stage where stamp duty on the Transfer needs to be
assessed and paid. Stamp duty is a government tax on the transfer of
property and must be paid before the Transfer can be registered.

The estimated stamp duty payable is Kenya Shillings [STAMP DUTY ESTIMATE]
(Kshs. [AMOUNT]), calculated at [4% / 2%] of the purchase price. This is
an estimate only — the final amount will be confirmed by KRA on the iTax
portal.

[If Purchaser pays stamp duty:] Stamp duty is payable by you as the
Purchaser. Please contact us to discuss the payment process via the KRA
iTax portal. We will guide you through the steps.

We will advise you once stamp duty has been paid and the Transfer documents
are ready.
```

---

### Milestone 7 — Transfer Documents Ready

```
We write to update you on progress with your [purchase / sale] of the
above property.

We are pleased to advise that the Transfer documents are now ready for
execution. The Transfer document (LRA 33 / LRA 63) is the formal
instrument that transfers ownership of the property from [Vendor's name]
to you.

We will require you to attend our office at your earliest convenience to
sign the Transfer documents in our presence. Kindly bring the following
when you come:

- Your original National Identity Card or Passport
- Your KRA PIN certificate
- [If married: your Marriage Certificate]
- [If company: a copy of the Board Resolution authorising this transaction]

Please contact us to schedule an appointment. The signing should take no
longer than 30 minutes.
```

---

### Milestone 8 — Lodged at Land Registry

```
We write to update you on progress with your [purchase / sale] of the
above property.

We are pleased to advise that we have lodged all the Transfer documents at
the Land Registry for registration. The Registry is processing the
registration and we expect to receive confirmation within approximately 10
business days.

Once the Transfer is registered, the property will be formally in
[Purchaser's name / your name], and we will arrange for the Completion
formalities and release of funds.

We will contact you as soon as registration is confirmed.
```

---

### Milestone 9 — Completion

```
We write to update you on the completion of your [purchase / sale] of the
above property.

We are delighted to confirm that the transaction has now been completed.
The Transfer has been registered at the Land Registry in your name, and
the new Certificate of Title has been issued.

[For Purchaser:] The property at [PROPERTY DESCRIPTION] is now registered
in your name. Your original Certificate of Title is being held securely in
our file and is available for collection at your convenience, or we can
arrange for it to be delivered to you.

[For Vendor:] The balance of the purchase price of Kenya Shillings
[BALANCE AMOUNT] has been released to you / will be remitted to your
account within [X] business days.

It has been a pleasure acting for you in this transaction. Please do not
hesitate to contact us if you require any further assistance.
```

## Drafting Notes

- Always address the letter to the correct client (Purchaser or Vendor). If the firm acts for both, write separate letters.
- Fill in all square bracket fields from the matter details in Airtable before sending.
- Always close with an offer to answer questions and the firm's contact details (on the letterhead).
- After sending, record in Airtable: Document Type = "Client Update", Status = "Sent", Date Sent = today's date.
