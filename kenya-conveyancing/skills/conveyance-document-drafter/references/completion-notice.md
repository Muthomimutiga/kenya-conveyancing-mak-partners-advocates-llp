# Completion Notice — Template

## Output Category

**Category A — Letterhead Document.** Begin output with:
`[LETTERHEAD DOCUMENT — Print on MAK & Partners Advocates LLP letterhead]`

**Typography:** Book Antiqua 12pt, single spaced, justified.

---

## When to Use

The Completion Notice is sent by the Purchaser's Advocates to the Vendor's Advocates formally notifying them that the Purchaser is ready and able to complete on the Completion Date, and specifying the arrangements for Completion.

Send this notice at least 5 business days before the Completion Date (or earlier as agreed) to allow the Vendor's Advocates to confirm their readiness and have all Completion Documents available.

**LSK Condition 8.2:** Completion shall take place at the Vendor's Advocates' office unless otherwise agreed.

---

## Completion Notice — Template

```
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[PRINT ON FIRM LETTERHEAD — MAK & PARTNERS ADVOCATES LLP]
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[DATE]

[VENDOR'S ADVOCATE FIRM NAME]
[ADDRESS]
[TOWN]

Dear [VENDOR'S ADVOCATE NAME / "Sirs"],

RE: [PROPERTY DESCRIPTION — e.g., "LAND REFERENCE NUMBER 209/4491 —
    PROPOSED TRANSFER FROM [VENDOR NAME] TO [PURCHASER NAME]"]

We act for [PURCHASER FULL NAME(S)] and refer to the above matter.

We write to formally notify you that our client, [PURCHASER FULL NAME(S)],
is ready, willing, and able to complete the purchase of the above property
on [COMPLETION DATE IN FULL].

COMPLETION ARRANGEMENTS

We propose the following arrangements for Completion:

Date:    [COMPLETION DATE — DAY, DATE MONTH YEAR]
Time:    [TIME, e.g., "10:00 a.m."]
Venue:   Your offices at [VENDOR'S ADVOCATE ADDRESS]

PURCHASE PRICE

The Purchase Price is Kenya Shillings [PURCHASE PRICE IN WORDS]
(Kshs. [PURCHASE PRICE IN FIGURES]).

Deposit paid: Kenya Shillings [DEPOSIT IN WORDS] (Kshs. [DEPOSIT IN
FIGURES]).

Balance to be paid on Completion: Kenya Shillings [BALANCE IN WORDS]
(Kshs. [BALANCE IN FIGURES]).

Our client will procure payment of the Balance of the Purchase Price by
RTGS transfer to your client account in accordance with the payment details
you provide, in cleared funds not later than three (3) Business Days before
the Completion Date, in accordance with Condition 8.3 of the Law Society
of Kenya Conditions of Sale (2015 Edition).

[OR — where undertaking is given:]

Our firm undertakes, as advocates for the Purchaser, that we hold Kenya
Shillings [BALANCE IN WORDS] (Kshs. [BALANCE IN FIGURES]) in our client
account and will release the same to you immediately upon receipt from you
of the Completion Documents listed below, duly executed and ready for
registration.

COMPLETION DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

We will require the following documents at Completion:

1. Original Certificate of Title / Certificate of Lease for the Property;
2. Duly executed Transfer instrument (LRA 33 [freehold] / LRA 63
   [leasehold]) in triplicate in favour of our client;
3. Original valid Rates Clearance Certificate for the Property (valid for
   at least twenty-one (21) days from the Completion Date) together with
   receipts evidencing payment for the last three (3) years;
[4. (Leasehold only) Original valid Land Rent Clearance Certificate and
   receipts evidencing payment for the last three (3) years;]
[5. Original NLC Consent to Transfer;]
[6. Duly executed LRA 58 Discharge of Charge from [CHARGEE NAME];]
7. Copies of latest utility bills paid to the Completion Date;
8. Copy of your client's National Identity Card / Passport;
9. Copy of your client's KRA PIN certificate;
10. Three (3) coloured passport-size photographs of your client;
[11. (If company) Copy of Certificate of Registration, CR12, Directors'
    Identity Cards, PIN certificates, and photographs;]
12. All consents necessary to effect registration of the Transfer.

Please confirm receipt of this notice and your client's readiness to
complete on the date stated above. Please also provide your client account
payment details for the RTGS transfer.

Yours faithfully,

_______________________
[SIGNING ADVOCATE]
MAK & PARTNERS ADVOCATES LLP
ADVOCATES FOR THE PURCHASER
```

## Drafting Notes

- Send via email and confirmed by post/courier.
- Remove numbered items in "Completion Documents Required" that are not applicable to the specific matter (e.g., remove NLC consent for freehold transactions).
- Confirm with the client that funds are available before sending this notice — the Purchaser is committing to Completion by this notice.
- After sending, create a record in Airtable Documents table: Document Type = "Completion Notice", Status = "Sent".
