# Agreement for Sale — Template

## Output Category

**Category A — Letterhead Document** (formal legal instrument, not a business letter).
Begin output with: `[LETTERHEAD DOCUMENT — Print on MAK & Partners Advocates LLP letterhead]`

**Typography:** Book Antiqua 12pt, single spaced, justified.

---

## Governing Standard Form

All Agreements for Sale prepared by MAK & Partners Advocates LLP incorporate the **Law Society of Kenya Conditions of Sale (2015 Edition)** by reference. The 2015 Edition superseded and repealed the 1989 Edition.

Where any Special Condition in the Agreement conflicts with the LSK Conditions of Sale, the Special Condition prevails (LSK Condition 25.8).

     shall not be required to repair or make further improvements.

6.3  Prior to the Completion Date, the Vendor shall point out the boundary beacons
     of the Property and shall reinstate or replace any missing beacon or beacons
     at no extra cost to the Purchaser.


7.   MOVABLES

     The sale includes no movables unless otherwise agreed in writing between the
     parties.


8.   VACANT POSSESSION

     The Property is sold with vacant possession. The Vendor shall deliver vacant
     possession of the Property to the Purchaser on the Completion Date following
     the payment of the Purchase Price in full to the Vendor's Advocates.


9.   BREACH OF AGREEMENT BY EITHER PARTY

9.1  If on the Completion Date the Purchaser is not ready, able or willing to
     complete the sale as required under the provisions of this Agreement, and
     without prejudice to any other rights or remedies available to the Vendor,
     the Vendor shall give the Purchaser twenty-one (21) days' notice in writing
     to comply with the Purchaser's obligations (time being of the essence). If
     the Purchaser shall fail to comply with such notice, the Vendor may at the
     Vendor's sole discretion:

     (a)  Extend the time for completion; or

     (b)  Rescind this Agreement by notice in writing to the Purchaser, whereupon
          the Purchaser shall forthwith return all documents delivered by or on
          behalf of the Vendor; and

     (c)  Forfeit ten percentum (10%) of the Purchase Price as liquidated damages,
          and the monies paid by the Purchaser to the Vendor less the forfeited
          amount shall only be refundable by the Vendor to the Purchaser within
          seven (7) days of such rescission.

9.2  If the Vendor shall fail to comply with any of the conditions hereof or if
     the transaction fails due to breach or fault on the part of the Vendor, the
     Purchaser shall give the Vendor twenty-one (21) days' notice in writing to
     remedy the same (time being of the essence). If the Vendor shall fail to
     comply with such notice, the Purchaser shall at the Purchaser's sole option
     be entitled to:

     (a)  Extend the time for completion; or

     (b)  Rescind this Agreement by notice in writing to the Vendor; and

     (c)  Demand an immediate full refund of all monies paid by the Purchaser to
          the Vendor, which shall be payable within seven (7) days of such demand.


10.  NON-MERGER

     This Agreement shall not merge on completion of the sale and purchase of the
     Property and completion shall not limit, cancel or extinguish the performance
     of any outstanding duties or obligations under this Agreement.


11.  COSTS, DUTIES AND FEES

11.1 Each party shall be responsible for the fees of its own legal advisers in
     connection with this Agreement and the sale and purchase of the Property.

11.2 Stamp duty and registration costs payable on the Transfer of the Property
     to the Purchaser shall be for the account of the Purchaser.

11.3 Any rates, rents, taxes, fees and any sums due to be paid by the Vendor to
     any relevant authority in respect of the Property on or before the Completion
     Date shall be paid by the Vendor. If the Vendor fails to pay such sums, the
     Purchaser may pay the same and recover them from the Purchase Price.


12.  GENERAL

12.1 No failure or delay to exercise any power, right or remedy by any party shall
     operate as a waiver of that right, power or remedy, and no single or partial

12.2 Each of the provisions of this Agreement is severable and distinct from the
     others and, if at any time one or more of these provisions is or becomes
     invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the validity, legality and enforceability
     of the remaining provisions shall not in any way be affected or impaired.

12.3 No amendment or variation to this Agreement shall be effectual or binding on
     the parties hereto unless it is in writing and duly executed by or on behalf

12.4 All provisions of this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect to
     the extent that they remain to be performed after Completion.


13.  INTENTION TO BE BOUND

     Each of the parties hereto hereby agrees and confirms that it has executed
     this Agreement with the intention to bind itself to the contents hereof.


14.  GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

14.1 This Agreement and any disputes or claims arising out of or in connection
     with its subject matter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
     the laws of the Republic of Kenya.

14.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be
     referred to and finally resolved by arbitration before a single arbitrator
     appointed by agreement of the parties or, failing agreement within fourteen
     (14) days, by the Chairman or Vice-Chairman for the time being of the Chartered
     Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch), in accordance with the rules of that
     Institute. The decision of the arbitrator shall be final and binding on the
     parties.


15.  NOTICES

15.1 Any notice required to be given by any party to the other under or in
     connection with this Agreement will only be effective:

     (a)  If by way of hand delivery, when delivered to the relevant address and
          upon signature of a delivery receipt;

     (b)  If by way of pre-paid first class registered post, seven (7) days after

     (d)  If by way of electronic communication, only when the email is actually

## Drafting Notes

- Fill all square bracket fields from party + property details collected at intake before outputting.
- Use the cover page format. It prints as page 1.
- The Spousal Consent block is required wherever the Property is or may be matrimonial property, or where any Vendor is or may be married. The spousal interest is an OVERRIDING interest under s.28 Land Registration Act 2012, so it binds whether or not an LRA 10 (Spouse's Interest) restriction is noted on the register — do NOT condition the consent on a register note. Confirm marital status at intake. Statutory basis: s.93 Land Registration Act 2012, read with s.12 Matrimonial Property Act 2013 (and, for a charge of a matrimonial home, s.79(3) Land Act 2012); constitutional anchor Article 45(3). Always include the block; it can be omitted at printing only if marital status is confirmed not to apply.
- For company Vendors or Purchasers: use the Company execution block. Include Directors' details and Board Resolution in Completion Documents list.
- Completion Date in Clause 5.1 must match the date recorded in Airtable and Outlook Calendar.
- Deposit in Clause 4.1 must equal 10% of Purchase Price unless a different amount is expressly agreed.
- Balance of Purchase Price = Purchase Price minus Deposit.
- For leasehold: include Clauses 5.2(iv) and 5.2(v) — NLC Rent Clearance and NLC Consent to Transfer.
- For agricultural land: include Clause 5.2(vi) — Land Control Board Consent.
- For charged property: include Clause 5.2(xiii) — Discharge of Charge.
- The firm's client-account details in Clause 4.1 ship as the loud placeholder `[MAK & PARTNERS CLIENT ACCOUNT — INSERT BEFORE SENDING]`. There is no MAK client/bank account on file in this template. Never substitute a guessed number and never leave a third-party firm's account: insert MAK & Partners' actual verified client-account details before the document is sent to any party. A wrong client-account figure reaching a party is unrecoverable.
