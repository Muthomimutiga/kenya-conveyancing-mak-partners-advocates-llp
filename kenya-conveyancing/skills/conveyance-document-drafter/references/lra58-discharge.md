# LRA 58 — Discharge of Charge

## Output Category

**Category B — Registry Document.** Begin output with:
`[REGISTRY DOCUMENT — No letterhead. File at Land Registry. Check title details before filing.]`

**Typography:** Book Antiqua 12pt, single spaced, justified.

---

## When to Use

LRA 58 is used when a charge (mortgage) over a property is fully repaid and the Chargee (lender/bank) releases its security interest. In a conveyancing transaction, this arises where:
- The Vendor has an outstanding mortgage that must be discharged at Completion before a clean title is transferred to the Purchaser; or
- The Purchaser is paying off the Vendor's lender as part of the transaction mechanics.

**Statutory basis:** discharge of charge under s.85 Land Act 2012; the prescribed form is LRA 58 (Land Registration (General) Regulations 2017, Sixth Schedule). Separately, s.59 Land Registration Act 2012 requires the lender's written consent before a transfer of charged land is registered. Lodging the LRA 58 Discharge simultaneously with the Transfer leaves the property unencumbered when the Transfer registers, so no separate consent is then needed; where the charge is to REMAIN, obtain the lender's written consent to the transfer (s.59 LRA).

**Timing:** Lodge the LRA 58 simultaneously with the Transfer instrument. The registry will register the discharge first, then the transfer.

## Drafting Notes

- The Discharge is signed by the CHARGEE (the bank / lender), NOT the Chargor (borrower). The Vendor's advocate must liaise with the lender to obtain a signed LRA 58 before Completion.
- Simultaneously lodge: where a Discharge is being lodged with a Transfer, present both at the same time. The Registrar processes the Discharge first.
- The lender's written consent to the transfer (s.59 LRA) is satisfied by the Discharge — you do not need a separate consent letter if the full Discharge is being given.
- Processing time: 7 business days (Reg. 32).
- The Discharge extinguishes the charge from the Encumbrances Section of the register (s.82 LRA).
