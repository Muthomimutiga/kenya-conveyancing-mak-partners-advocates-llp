# Kenya Conveyancing Law: Reference

Full statutory and procedural reference for the Kenya Conveyancing Manager.

Primary authority: the Land Registration Act 2012, the Land Act 2012 (Cap 280), the Land Control Act (Cap 302), and the Stamp Duty Act (Cap 480), read together with the Registration of Titles practice. Verify current provisions against the Kenya Law MCP (kenya-law) before relying on any figure in a live matter.

This reference summarises the key statutory rules a Kenyan conveyancing matter turns on: priority, the stamp duty gateway, indefeasibility, freehold vs leasehold treatment, LCB consent, execution requirements, registry procedure, the subdivision rule, and processing times. It is a quick-reference summary, not exhaustive. Verify any figure or provision against the Kenya Law MCP before relying on it in a live matter.

---

## Key Rules Summary for Quick Reference

### Priority
- Priority = order of presentation to the Land Registry, not date of instrument (s.36(5) LRA).
- Lodging LRA 84 with proprietor's consent creates a 14-day protection window (ss.36(8)–(9) LRA).

### Stamp Duty Gateway
- No instrument is accepted for registration unless stamped (s.46 LRA). Absolute, no exceptions.
- Urban: 4%. Rural: 2%. Base = higher of consideration or market value.

### Indefeasibility
- Registered title is absolute (s.25 LRA). Overriding interests are the main exception (s.28 LRA).

### Freehold vs Leasehold
- Freehold: LRA 33 transfer, no NLC consent, no rent clearance, LCB consent only if agricultural.
- Leasehold: LRA 63 transfer, NLC consent required (ss.55–56 LRA), Land Rent Clearance Certificate required.

### LCB Consent
- Required for all dealings in agricultural land (Land Control Act Cap. 302).
- Without it: the dealing is void. Not applicable to urban/commercial/industrial land.

### Execution
- All instruments: signed by both parties + verified by qualified advocate (s.44 LRA + Reg. 88).
- All forms in English, legible, paginated. Attesting witness signs on same page as executing party (Reg. 87).

### Registry Procedure
- Hours: Monday–Friday 9:00am–1:00pm, 2:00pm–4:00pm.
- Instruments in two counterparts (Reg. 22). LRA 9 in quadruplicate. LRA 84 in triplicate.
- Fees: electronic payment or banker's cheque only. No cash (Reg. 36).
- No transfer of deceased proprietor's land until confirmation of grant (Reg. 55).

### Subdivision
- A parcel cannot be transferred in parts until the subdivision is first approved and registered (s.42 LRA).

### Key Processing Times
| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Official search | 5 business days |
| Transfer / charge / lease | 10 business days |
| Discharge / POA | 7 business days |
